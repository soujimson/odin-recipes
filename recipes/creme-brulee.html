Creme Brulee

Description

A creme brulee is a vanilla custard topped with caramelized sugar for a glossy crunch.

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups heavy whipping cream
  • 5 Egg yolks
  • 1/2 Cup Sugar
  • Salt
  • 1 Tsp of any form of vanilla

Steps

  1. Heat your heavy cream until steaming, add your vanilla and remove from heat
  2. Whisk in your egg yolks, sugar and salt in a separate bowl.
  3. Strain the custard through a mesh sieve into 6 ramekins and place in baking dish.
  4. Add boiling water to the baking dish and bake the creme brulee at 325 degrees farenheit for 30-35 minutes.
  5. Cool on a wire rack, cover them, and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
  6. Sprinkle sugar on each ramekin and blow torch the sugar until it melts into a glassy shell.
