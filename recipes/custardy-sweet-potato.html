Custardy Sweet Potatoes

Description

To make any sweet potato into a custardy treat, you only need one ingredient and some appliances!

Ingredients

  • Sweet potato of your choosing
  • (Optional) Salt
  • (Optional) Black pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 400 degrees farenheit.
  2. Wash your potatoes and dry them.
  3. Poke each potato all around with a knife or fork to create vents for steam.
  4. Wrap you potatoes in aluminum foil and toss them in the oven for 45 minutes.
  5. Once 45 minutes are up, turn off the oven but do not open it. Leave the potatoes to sit unbothered for 1-2 hours.
  6. Enjoy as is or add some salt and pepper for a savory kick.
