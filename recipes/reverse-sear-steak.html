Reverse Seared Steak

Description

A reverse seared steak is first cooked in the oven and then hard seared on a pan to acheive a nice crust.

This method helps achieve a hard crust without worrying about overcooking your steak.

Ingredients

  • A few cloves of garlic
  • Two tablespoons of butter
  • One steak of your choice
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Oil with a high smoke point

Steps

  1. Salt your steak, place it on a steel wire rack above a baking tray, and leave in your fridge uncovered for at least 6 hours.
  2. Preheat your oven to 250 degrees fahrenheit.
  3. Bake your steak on the wire rack and try for 25-30 minutes or until the internal reaches 10 degrees fahrenheit lower than your desired doneness.
  4. Let it rest for 10 minutes and preheat your pan.
  5. Heat up a little bit of oil until it shimmers and lay your steak in the pan.
  6. Flip the steak after 1-2 minutes or until it releases.
  7. After 1-2 minutes, lower the heat and add in your butter and cloves of garlic.
  8. Baste your steak for 1 minute and pull it off the pan to let it rest for 8-10 minutes.
  9. Dig in!
